Two people, three contracts

Two working styles, and what each bargain asks of each person. Hover a bargain to isolate it.

The Engager

Brings

Appetite and questions; best thinking happens out loud, in the room.

Engages by

Probing, combining, testing ideas early.

Needs

Something to react to, and permission to question while the clay is wet.

The Preparer

Brings

Finished thinking; best work happens before the room.

Engages by

Presenting researched ideas, and expecting the same preparation.

Needs

The work respected, questions that build rather than demolish, and time to think before responding.

AssimilateWear the mask

The Engager

Bring finished-looking ideas. Swallow your questions.

The Preparer

Keep polishing. Accept only praise.

Costs: The Engager drains performing; the Preparer learns nothing.

ContainGive the acceptable part

The Engager

Ask only safe questions. Hold the rest.

The Preparer

Share conclusions. Skip the workings.

Costs: Both ration themselves; the work is never tested.

ChannelMove toward each other

The Engager

Do the reading. Question to build.

The Preparer

Share materials early. Hold the idea loosely.

Pays: Both spend on the work; ideas become ours.
