A boss and a botanist

Same team, different theory of control. Hover a row to isolate it.

The boss

Moves pieces with precision, assumed to be the most knowledgeable in the room.

The botanist

Assembles diverse teams, knowing the people closest to the work hold the insights.

The boss

Hands down directives and dictates outcomes.

The botanist

Sets the brief and the standards, then gives room to move.

The boss

Enforces rules and weeds out dissent.

The botanist

Prunes to create space, weeding only what threatens the garden.

The boss

Demands the bloom on schedule.

The botanist

Holds the conditions; the growing stays the plant's job.
