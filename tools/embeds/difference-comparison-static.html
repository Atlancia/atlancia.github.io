Same difference, three bargains

What the room asks of you when you arrive different, and what each bargain costs.

Assimilate

Wear the mask

Definition

Act different than you are so the room stays comfortable.

Impact

Performing costs energy, and your contribution shrinks to whatever fits the window the team allows.

Contain

Give the acceptable part

Definition

Offer what the room will take and hold back the rest.

Impact

Nothing false, but the best of what you bring never enters the work.

Channel

Move toward each other

Definition

Agree you are different and aim it at the work instead.

Impact

You learn how to work together, and over time it compounds: more acceptance, more value, outcomes neither side could reach alone.

Same difference. Three bargains. Only one lets the work change.
