The Knowledge Boundary

Most complex at the top, least at the bottom. The boundary sits between Complex and Complicated.

Beyond the boundary

Chaotic Unknown No cause and effect to find in the moment.No stable solution exists, so rapid experimentation is the quality system.
Complex Unknowable Information is created through work.No optimum holds still, so experimentation and new perspectives are the method.
Knowledge Boundary

Below the boundary

Complicated Knowable Knowable by experts through analysis.Several good solutions, one optimum that holds: apply good practice.
Simple Known Cause and effect are clear.Apply best practice and check conformance: training reliably reproduces the result.
