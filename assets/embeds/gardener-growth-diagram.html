Growth conditions

The weather changes; the gardener holds three things

Light, soil, room to move. Each holds, but each can also be commanded past its limit.

Condition

Clarity of the brief

Held by the manager

A clear brief gives the work its direction, not its outcome.

Standards of the work

Held by agreement

Shared standards hold the team together when conditions change.

Room to move

Held only partly

Space can be offered; it cannot be forced. Past the line, the bloom refuses.

Limit

The meter stops

Nothing above is for sale

The growing stays the plant's job. The command stops at the line.

The gardener holds the weather. The growth stays beyond command.
